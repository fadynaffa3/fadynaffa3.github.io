About

Senior Software Engineer and Technical Co-Founder with 13+ years of experience building, architecting, and scaling enterprise-grade products from zero to scale. My career bridges the gap between deep infrastructure engineering and production-grade AI system design for global market leaders, including Contentful, Sensor Tower, Oyster, and Toptal. At ZenHR, Oyster and Toptal I have led and mentored engineering teams of 10 or more.

I have also built payment systems end to end: Stripe, PayPal and payroll-provider integrations, ledgers and money tracking, refunds, invoicing and automatic payments, at ZenHR and at Oyster.

Currently at Toptal, I architect and engineer AI-driven products. My work centers on building reliable, production-ready LLM pipelines for complex tasks: translating raw data into structured job posts, analyzing hiring feasibility, expanding semantic search terms, and driving candidate screening for an agentic sourcing service.

To me, AI engineering is as much about the hard systems engineering around the model as the prompts themselves. I specialize in designing the robust wrappers that make generative AI enterprise-ready: enforcing strict structured outputs, implementing precise cost caps, managing idempotent background jobs, building predictable retry policies, and writing test suites to guarantee behavioral guardrails hold under load.

I value stable, deeply tested systems, deliberate technical debt management, and engineering cultures that maintain a high shipping velocity without compromising system stability.
